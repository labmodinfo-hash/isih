    <h1 class="post-title">ISOF — Institute of Semiotics of Images and Folklore</h1>
    <p class="post-lead">
        The Russian-language research division of ISIH specializing in visual semiotics, Japanese animation (anime), graphic narratives, and folklore motifs.
    </p>

    <div class="post-content">
        <h2>About the Division</h2>
        <p>
            Founded as a dedicated platform for studying contemporary visual culture, the <strong>Institute of Semiotics of Images and Folklore (ISOF)</strong> operates alongside ISIH to bridge regional Slavic and Russian-language scholarship with international comparative semiotics.
        </p>

        <h2>Core Research Areas</h2>
        <p>
            <strong>Visual Codes & Graphic Grammar:</strong> Color symbolism, emotion markers (<em>manpu</em>), spatial framing, and character design archetypes in Japanese animation.
        </p>
        <p>
            <strong>Folklore & Sacred Motifs:</strong> Shinto ritual boundaries, Buddhist imagery, yokai demonology, and the transformation of traditional myths in modern pop culture.
        </p>
