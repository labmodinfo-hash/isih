    <div class="post-meta">About the Institute</div>
    <h1 class="post-title">International Semiotics Institute for Heritage (ISIH)</h1>
    <p class="post-lead">
        An independent interdisciplinary research initiative dedicated to studying cultural heritage, mythology, and contemporary visual media as living sign systems.
    </p>

    <div class="post-content">
        <h2>Mission & Scope</h2>
        <p>
            The International Semiotics Institute for Heritage (ISIH) brings together scholars working at the intersection of semiotics, comparative mythology, religious studies, and visual anthropology. Our goal is to trace how ancient symbolic structures, ritual aesthetics, and archetypal narratives persist and transform across historical epochs and modern media.
        </p>

        <h2>Key Areas of Inquiry</h2>
        <p>
            <strong>Cross-Cultural & South Asian Studies:</strong> Investigating classical Indian aesthetics (such as <em>Rasa</em> theory), sacred iconography, epic traditions, and historical cultural dialogues across Eurasia.
        </p>
        <p>
            <strong>Visual Semiotics & Contemporary Media:</strong> Decoding color symbolism, graphic emotion markers, spatial framing, and mythological motifs in contemporary animation and digital culture.
        </p>

        <h2>Institutional Network</h2>
        <p>
            ISIH operates in close collaboration with its Russian-language visual culture division, the <strong>Institute of Semiotics of Images and Folklore (<a href="https://isof.ru" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="color: var(--accent-red); text-decoration: underline;">ISOF</a>)</strong>, bridging regional academic research with an international scholarly audience.
        </p>
