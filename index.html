ISIH Lexicon • Term of the Day
Rasa
रस — «aesthetic essence or emotional flavor»

A foundational concept in classical Indian aesthetics (Natya Shastra) denoting the emotional atmosphere evoked in the audience through dramatic, poetic, or visual sign systems. It bridges ancient ritual performance with contemporary cross-cultural visual storytelling.

Cultural Heritage as a Living Sign System
